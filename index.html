A completely unserious privacy policy

Your sentence never leaves this tab. The relic is generated with a small deterministic hash in your browser. There is no upload, account, analytics on the text, or server call involved in making or exporting it. The shared site chrome may load page analytics, but this tool does not transmit the specimen.

Why does the same sentence make the same object?

The text, selected format, strange-o-meter, and reroll number are hashed locally. That hash chooses the date, origin, status, field note, and signal pattern. Nothing is random behind your back, and nothing is stored after you close the page.

FAQ

What file do I actually get?

An SVG image (future-relic-<id>.svg) that opens in any browser or image viewer and scales to any size without losing quality.

The share or copy button didn't do anything

Some browsers and operating systems block the clipboard or system share sheet for this kind of content. Download the SVG instead — it always works and produces the exact same result.

Is this trying to predict the future?

No. It's a novelty generator that turns your sentence into a strange, consistent-looking artifact for fun. Nothing here is a forecast, analysis, or claim about what will actually happen.
